Developer & ATC Controller

I'm notdeath—a systems builder who enjoys taking apart complex problems and putting them back together as clean, reliable applications. My work lives at the intersection of aviation software and desktop tooling.

As an active VATSIM ATC Controller, managing simulated radar environments has sharpened my sense of precision, process structure, and critical thinking under pressure. I bring that same discipline to every line of code—structured schemas, clean APIs, and applications that just work.

My core stack is Node.js, Electron, and SQLite for offline-first desktop tools, paired with Python for automation and workflow scripting. I run Arch Linux with KDE Plasma because I like having full control over my environment.
